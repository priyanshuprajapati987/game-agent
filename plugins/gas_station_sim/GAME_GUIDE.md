# Gas Station Simulator - Game Guide for Agent

## Overview
Gas Station Simulator is a first-person management game where you run a gas station in the desert. You work for the mob and must keep your business running while managing multiple tasks simultaneously.

## Core Gameplay Loop
1. **Customers arrive** at gas pumps
2. **Pump gas** for them (minigame)
3. **They come to register** to pay
4. **Keep floor clean** (desert sand everywhere)
5. **Stock shelves** with products
6. **Fix cars** in workshop
7. **Deal with Dennis** (troublemaker)
8. **Manage employees** and deliveries

## Game Elements

### Gas Pumping (Minigame)
- Grab pump handle
- Position nozzle at car
- Hold trigger to pump
- **Watch the speed meter** - keep needle in green zone
- **Tap Shift** for small amounts
- **Release early** - needle overshoots after long holds
- Stop when tank is full

### Register/Checkout
- Customers place items on counter
- Click to scan each item
- Check total amount
- Press E to take payment
- Press F to give receipt

### Cleaning
- **Floor dirt meter** in top-left shows cleanliness
- Equip broom (click broom icon)
- Drag across dirty floor to sweep
- Empty trash cans before they overflow
- Carry trash to dumpster outside
- Throw with right-click

### Workshop Repairs
- Cars arrive with damage
- Activate ramp (click button)
- Scan car for red-outlined broken parts
- Replace parts from inventory
- Paint scratches (hold and drag)
- Lower ramp when done

### Dennis the Troublemaker
- Appears randomly, usually when busy
- Sprays graffiti on walls
- **HIGHEST PRIORITY** - deal immediately
- Throw trash at him (right-click)
- Keep trash equipped nearby

### Employee Management
- Use computer to hire/manage
- Max 5 employees
- Roles: Cashier, Pump Attendant, Cleaner, Mechanic
- Pay them before assigning tasks
- They work 8-hour shifts

### Deliveries
- Order via computer
- Truck arrives at warehouse
- Open door, unload, **CLOSE THE DOOR**
- If door stays open, items get stolen!

## Priority System (for Agent)

### Critical (Drop everything)
1. Dennis spraying graffiti
2. Fuel pump empty
3. Customers leaving

### High
4. Customer at pump
5. Customer at register
6. Car in workshop

### Medium
7. Floor very dirty
8. Trash cans full
9. Delivery arrived

### Low
10. Stocking shelves
11. Decorating
12. Employee management

## Tips for Success
1. **Floor cleanliness** directly affects customer count
2. **Empty trash** before green fumes appear
3. **Pump carefully** - overshooting wastes fuel
4. **Stock variety** - more product types = more sales
5. **Party bus** = stock everything, they buy it all
6. **Close station** when doing maintenance
7. **Reset button** fixes stuck cars/trucks
8. **Order parts** in advance for workshop
9. **Keeptrash handy** for Dennis
10. **Close warehouse doors** after delivery

## Common Mistakes to Avoid
- Leaving trash cans to overflow
- Ignoring Dennis too long
- Forgetting to close warehouse door
- Over-pumping fuel (waste)
- Not stocking before party bus
- Leaving station open during maintenance
